SPY HUNT FIELD MANUAL

OVERVIEW

A secret transmission has been intercepted. One operative is a Spy trying to steal blueprints. The rest are Agents trying to root out the traitor.

GAMEPLAY

  • Agents are briefed on the Location and their specific Role.
  • The Spy has no briefing and only knows they are the Spy.
  • Operatives take turns asking one another questions to probe for suspicious details without directly naming the location (which would give it away to the Spy).
  • Digital Mode: You ask questions by choosing topics (e.g. noise levels, clothing) and answering using logical indicators. AI agents will evaluate answers and adjust their suspicion metrics.

WIN CONDITIONS

  • Agents Win: By successfully voting out the Spy in an Accusation, or if the Spy guesses the location incorrectly.
  • Spy Wins: By guessing the correct location, or if the Agents vote out an innocent Agent during an Accusation, or if the timer runs out.

ACCUSATION & VOTING

  • Any player can accuse someone of being the Spy.
  • An accusation triggers a vote. If the accused player receives a majority of GUILTY votes, the game ends.
  • AIs vote based on their internal suspicion scores, which rise when players give answers that conflict with the location attributes.
